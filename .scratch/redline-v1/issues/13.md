## What to build

On a document's page, the reader can ask plain-language questions and get answers built only from what that document says.

`answerQuestion(text, question) → { answer } | { declined: true, reason }`, independent of `analyzeDocument`.

- **Grounded or silent:** where the text doesn't support an answer, it says the document doesn't say. No hedging and no reasoning toward a guess — unlike a flag, an answer has no source sentence to lean on (ADR-0007's plain-or-omit principle applies here).
- **Remedy questions are declined, with a reason** (ADR-0003) — e.g. "they haven't paid me, what are my options" — even on an already-signed document. The boundary is the question asked, not the document's age.
- **Renewal reads are answered normally** — "what should I negotiate differently next time" on the same signed document.
- **Requests to draft filler language for a gap are declined, with a reason** (ADR-0014).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A question box on the document view submits to `answerQuestion` and shows the answer or the decline reason
- [ ] Test: a question the fixture text doesn't cover returns a "the document doesn't say" answer, not a guess
- [ ] Test: a remedy-question fixture is declined with a stated reason, never answered
- [ ] Test: a gap-fill-request fixture ("what should I put here instead" for an absent term) is declined with a stated reason
- [ ] Test: a renewal-read question on the same document is answered, not declined
- [ ] Answers contain no hedged wording
