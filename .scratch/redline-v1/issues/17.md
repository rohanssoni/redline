# #17 Landing page: a sample agreement turning into ranked flags

Labels: ready-for-agent

## Parent

#1 — Redline v1: full-scope spec

## What to build

A public landing page for the reader PRD.md names: a freelancer or independent contractor about to sign a client's agreement. It demonstrates one thing, a sample agreement turning into ranked flags with each flag showing the exact sentence it came from, and it offers one action: try it on a document.

This ticket also scaffolds the Next.js app the rest of the build grows from: TypeScript, plain CSS, and next/font, with no other dependencies.

The page follows the landing surface brief's direction contract (visual world: Tracked Changes). The sample agreement is synthetic and visibly labelled as a sample. Its flags are held to ADR-0001 like real output: every source sentence appears word for word in the sample text.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Next.js app is scaffolded with TypeScript, plain CSS, and next/font, and no other dependencies
- [ ] The landing page is public and needs no sign-in
- [ ] The demonstration uses a synthetic freelance services agreement, visibly labelled as a sample wherever a visitor could mistake it for real
- [ ] The demonstration shows flags ranked by severity, each with its source sentence
- [ ] A test asserts that every source sentence in the demonstration appears verbatim in the sample agreement text
- [ ] There is exactly one action, trying it on a document, and it leads to the no-account try
- [ ] One plain line next to the action says Redline shows what the document says and doesn't give legal advice
- [ ] The page claims nothing the scope excludes: no verdict on whether to sign, no legal advice, no scanned or photographed documents, no document types beyond a freelancer's client agreement
- [ ] No prices, customers, testimonials, or quotes appear
- [ ] Every line of copy has been run through the humanizer skill before commit
- [ ] The build passes the finish review, and DESIGN.md records the visual world
