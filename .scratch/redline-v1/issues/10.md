## What to build

A signed-in reader can build and edit their own list of **red lines** — terms they've decided in advance they won't accept. The list belongs to the reader, persists across every document they analyze, and is readable by the analysis on each run.

This slice is the list itself; making red lines drive the analysis is the next red-line ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A reader can add, edit, and delete red lines in plain language
- [ ] The list persists across sessions and across documents
- [ ] A reader can only see and change their own red lines
- [ ] The analysis entry point receives the reader's current red lines (the `redLines` argument of `analyzeDocument`)
