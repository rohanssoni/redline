## What to build
`analyzeDocument` returns real `gaps[]`. A **gap** is a term the agreement should
contain but doesn't. It has no source sentence, because there is no sentence. It
is phrased as a whole-document claim ("This agreement contains no late-payment
term.") and is visibly labelled a gap, never a flag.

Severity governs order; **type governs what an item may claim**. A high-severity
gap can outrank a low-severity flag, but prominence never turns a gap into a flag.
Flags and gaps interleave in one list sorted by descending `severity`.

Make the distinction hold in the types, not just at render time: a gap should be
structurally incapable of carrying a source sentence, so a future ticket cannot
accidentally give it one. This is the ADR-0005 guarantee.

The fixture's gaps are `no-late-payment-term` and `no-liability-cap` in
`tests/fixtures/adhesion-agreement.json`.

## Tests
- The named test from the spec: a gap never carries a source sentence and never
  appears in `flags[]`, **at any severity, including a fixture where the gap is
  the highest-severity item**. Build that case explicitly — stub a response whose
  top-severity item is a gap.
- Flags and gaps come back interleaved in one descending-severity order.
- Gaps are persisted with the analysis result and survive a reload.
