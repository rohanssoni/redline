## What to build
A reader can dismiss a flag they have read and decided isn't a problem **for
them**. A dismissed flag is visually set aside but still recoverable, and the
dismissal persists across sessions.

A nonzero dismissal rate is **expected by design** — it is the cost of favouring
recall (ADR-0006). The metric exists to tell whether the confidence threshold or
the red-line matching needs retuning. Nothing in the reader-facing copy should
make dismissing feel like a mistake or like correcting the product.

Dismissals are recorded with **whether the flag was red-line-triggered**, and the
product owner can view the two rates **separately**. A reader dismissing their own
stated red line is a different signal from dismissing a borderline flag the model
raised on its own — if the two get averaged together, the metric is useless.

**Gaps are not part of this metric.** A gap is not dismissible in this ticket.

Owner-facing reporting is gated the same way #7's is — not reachable by an
ordinary signed-in reader.

## Tests
- Dismissing a flag sets it aside, persists, and can be undone.
- A dismissal records whether the flag was red-line-triggered.
- The two rates are computed separately, and a mixed set of dismissals produces
  the right number for each — not one rate reported twice.
- Gaps never enter the metric.
