## What to build
A per-flag soft/firm **stance** control, defaulting to soft. Switching to firm
calls `draftCounterOffer(flag, 'firm')` **for that flag only**, on demand, the
moment the reader switches — never pre-generated (ADR-0012). There is a visible
wait, so there is a visible loading state; the app shell brief lists "firm draft
loading or failed" as a state to design.

Once generated, the firm version is **persisted and reused**: switching back and
forth, or reopening the document later, must not call the model again.

Stance is per flag. Switching one flag never changes another's.

Generation failure leaves the soft counter-offer intact and tells the reader, in
copy that says what happened without blaming them.

## Tests
- The named test from the spec: **immediately after `analyzeDocument`, only the
  soft counter-offer exists for a flag; the firm one is absent until
  `draftCounterOffer(flag, 'firm')` is called.** Assert on the stub's call log.
- A generated firm version is reused — a second switch, and a reload of the
  stored document, make no further model call.
- Switching one flag's stance leaves every other flag's stance unchanged.
- A failing firm generation leaves the soft counter-offer intact and surfaces an
  error, not an empty counter-offer.
