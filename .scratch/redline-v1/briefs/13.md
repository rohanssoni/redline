## What to build
`answerQuestion(text, question, deps) → { answer } | { declined: true, reason }`,
a seam **independent of `analyzeDocument`**, invoked whenever the reader asks
something.

- **Grounded or silent.** Answers are built only from what that document says.
  Where the text doesn't support an answer, it says **the document doesn't say**
  — it does not reason toward a plausible guess.
- **No hedging here.** Unlike a flag, an answer has no source sentence of its own
  to lean on, so ADR-0007's plain-or-omit rule applies to this seam: state it
  plainly, or decline. "This could possibly mean…" is a defect in this seam.
- **Remedy questions are declined, with a stated reason** (ADR-0003). "They
  haven't paid me, what are my options" is declined **even on an already-signed
  document**. The boundary is the question asked, not the document's age.
- **Renewal reads are answered normally.** "What should I negotiate differently
  next time", on that same signed document, is a **renewal read** and gets a
  normal answer. Getting this boundary right in both directions is the point of
  the ticket — over-declining is as wrong as under-declining.
- **A request to draft filler language for a gap is declined, with a reason**
  (ADR-0014). The reader is never handed invented clause language.

When declining, the product **says why** rather than going quiet. The reason is
reader-facing copy: plain, not apologetic, and it must not imply the reader asked
something improper.

Regulatory note from `PRODUCT.md` and ADR-0003: nothing here may read as legal
advice. That constrains the decline copy specifically.

## Tests (stub model client; the fixtures are the documents)
- A question the fixture text doesn't cover returns a "the document doesn't say"
  answer, not a guess.
- A **remedy-question** fixture is declined with a stated reason, never answered.
- A **gap-fill-request** fixture ("what should I put here instead" for an absent
  term — the adhesion fixture has no late-payment term) is declined with a
  stated reason.
- A **renewal-read** question on the same document is answered, not declined.
- Answers contain no hedged wording. Assert it.
