## What to build
The **zero-flag rate** — the share of analyzed documents returning a clean read —
compared against a baseline, so a severity filter that starts silently suppressing
everything gets caught. This is a **health metric, not a target to move**. Say that
in the reporting copy.

The numbers are given and **must not be re-derived or tuned**:
- Compare against a **fixed 20% baseline** until switchover.
- Switchover fires at whichever comes first: **500 analyzed documents**, or
  **90 days since launch**.
- After switchover, compare against a **rolling window**.

Both comparison logics must exist and actually swap. Put the switchover decision
in a pure, directly testable function that takes the document count and the
elapsed time and returns which comparison is in force — do not bury it in a query.

"Launch" needs a concrete definition; pick one (e.g. a recorded launch timestamp
in config or a metrics row), record it in your report, and make it injectable so
the tests can drive time without waiting 90 days. Do not use the real clock in
tests.

Every completed analysis records whether it was a clean read. The rate and its
current baseline are viewable by the **product owner, not by readers** — gate it
so an ordinary signed-in reader cannot reach it. Say how you gated it in your
report; if that needs a Supabase role or a flag, write it into the migration
under `supabase/migrations/`.

## Tests
- The switchover fires on **document count first** (500 documents reached well
  inside 90 days).
- The switchover fires on **elapsed time first** (90 days passed with few
  documents).
- Before switchover the comparison is against a fixed 20%; after, against the
  rolling window.
- A clean read and a flagged analysis each record the right thing.
