## What to build
`draftCounterOffer(flag, stance, deps)` returning the drafted text — a seam
**independent of** `analyzeDocument`. Called once per flag at analysis time with
`stance: 'soft'`.

Constraints that are the point of the ticket:
- A counter-offer **rewrites only the flagged clause**, never the whole agreement.
- It **never argues against terms the document doesn't contain** — it inherits
  ADR-0001's grounding. It is anchored to the one clause it rewrites, and that
  clause's source sentence is what anchors it.
- **Gaps never get a counter-offer, under any circumstance** (ADR-0014). Make this
  structural: `draftCounterOffer` should not be *callable* with a gap, by type.
  A gap has no drafted remedy, no filler language, however severe it is.

The reader can copy a counter-offer to the clipboard. (Copy is also the "sent"
signal #16 will track — leave a clean seam for that, but do not build the metric.)

Soft counter-offers are persisted with the analysis result.

Only the **soft** version exists after analysis. Do not pre-generate firm — that
is #9, and ADR-0012 specifically forbids pre-generating it.

## Tests
- Exactly one soft draft per flag at analysis time, and `draftCounterOffer` is
  called with `'soft'`. Count the stub's calls.
- **Zero** drafts for gaps — no gap displays counter-offer language. Use the
  adhesion fixture, which has two gaps.
- A counter-offer addresses only its own flagged clause (assert it is anchored to
  that flag's source sentence, and that a response straying into another clause
  is not silently accepted).
