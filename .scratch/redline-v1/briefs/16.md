## What to build
The product owner can see how often counter-offers are **sent** — the primary
success metric — split by **stance** (soft vs. firm).

"Sent" is measured as **the reader copying a counter-offer**. The app cannot
observe a message actually leaving, so copy is a proxy. This was decided when #1
was broken down and is not open for reinterpretation. What it requires of you:
**record it as a copy event and label it that way in the reporting**, so nobody
downstream mistakes the proxy for confirmed sends. If the reporting says "sent"
with no qualification, this ticket is not done.

Copying a counter-offer records an event with the flag, the stance copied, and
the document.

Repeated copies of the same counter-offer in the same stance must be
distinguishable from distinct sends: report **unique flag+stance** alongside raw
copy count. A reader who copies the same draft four times while pasting it into
an email is one send, not four, and the raw count still matters for noticing that
pattern.

Owner-facing reporting is gated the same way #7's and #15's are.

## Tests
- Copying records an event carrying the flag, the stance, and the document.
- The owner view splits soft from firm and the two numbers are right for a mixed
  set of events.
- Four copies of one flag+stance report as 1 unique and 4 raw — assert both.
- The reporting labels the metric as copy-based.
